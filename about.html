A BETTER WAY TO PLAY

Big performance.
Open to everyone.

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was founded in Sandton in 2023 by gamer Jason Naidoo. The idea came from a familiar frustration: older hardware and low frame rates can hold back a player, while buying a high-end gaming setup is out of reach for many.

We built a place where players can get on high-spec PCs and current-gen consoles, explore VR and racing simulators, and get coaching without the cost of owning all that equipment. Whether you're learning the ropes or preparing for competition, there's room to play, improve and connect here.
